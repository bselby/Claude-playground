# Jewel Design Studio — Brand Context

## The Business
Jewel Design Studio is an interior design studio based in Walthamstow,
east London. Founded by Ben Selby. Works with homeowners across London
and the South East to create interiors that are practical, warm, and
beautiful.

## Positioning
Down to earth and pragmatic. Not intimidating or aspirational in a cold
way. Design that solves real problems for real people. A home should feel
like a hug. Soft, calm, cosy.

## Tone of Voice
Warm, reassuring, competent. Like a brilliant friend who happens to be an
expert. Never uses the words "luxury" or "bespoke." Speaks to people, not
at them. Conversational but knowledgeable. Never preachy. Occasionally
dry and funny. Always honest.

Ben's personal voice: down to earth, Walthamstow-based, dad to a toddler,
loves sport and music, cares deeply about craft and getting things right.
Not flashy. Not corporate. Real.

## The Three Audiences

### Audience 1 — Full Design Clients (Primary)
Busy London/South East professionals. Cash rich, time poor. Ready to
invest. Come via word of mouth. Before finding Ben: overwhelmed,
paralysed, have been putting the project off for months. After: calm,
clear, living in a home they love.

### Audience 2 — Consultation Clients (Secondary)
Aspirational homeowners not yet ready for full design. TikTok/Instagram
driven. Price point £145-295. Natural pipeline to Audience 1.

### Audience 3 — Digital Product Buyers (Tertiary)
Social followers wanting practical guides and resources. Stan Store
journey. Entry point to the wider ecosystem.

## Completed Projects

### Walthamstow
4-bedroom Victorian terrace, full renovation, kitchen extension, loft
conversion. Clients loved colour but were nervous it wouldn't feel
cohesive. Solved with transitional spaces and related tones. Near-black
and dusty pink kitchen with Lacanche range and quartzite island. Deep
blue bedroom with crystal chandelier. Terracotta bedroom with lacquered
ceiling. Sage green loft conversion. Took nearly 3 years, clients lived
in throughout. They were nervous about colour at the start. They aren't
any more.

### Wimbledon
Loft conversion — bedroom, dressing room, en-suite. Brief: calming
retreat with pops of colour. Burgundy bespoke joinery in dressing room.
Brass and marble en-suite with herringbone tiles. Started as spatial
planning brief, became full design project.

### Tooting Bec
Bathroom only. Brief: boutique hotel feel with a statement basin. Couple
were expecting their first baby. Purple-veined marble double sink. Dark
zellige tiles. Brass pendants. They now bathe their daughter in the basin.
